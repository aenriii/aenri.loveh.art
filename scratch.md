[ openrx ] 

openrx is a harm reduction, dosing, and sourcing resource for grey-market
pharmaceuticals, focusing on DIY HRT for trans people, and DIY GLP-1 medication
for those who are unable to access it for any reason. it's built with svelte,
including a small custom localization framework. contributions are always
welcome! contact me through matrix or signal to talk about what you can add, or
how you can otherwise help.

the .info domain seeks to be the first of multiple resources under that name,
hopefully evolving into an international mutual aid network to connect people
with life-saving medication regardless of who they are and what they need.

[[ genesis ]]

genesis is a multiplatform multi-service chat app, launching a v0.1 with
support for IRC, and a v1.0 with support for Matrix and XMPP. it uses an
advanced security architecture to make sure your data is safe on-device and
off device.

eventually it hopes to grow into a discord-like chat service based on signal's
server architecture, security design, and protocol in order to provide more
secure communications than previously possible in this chat app format.

development is slow / paused right now, i lost motivation for it a little bit

or bounties for features are well appreciated! contact me on matrix or signal
about any contributions you'd like to make.

[[ mewsic ]]

mewsic is a multiplatform multi-service music player and local library manager
with first-party support for youtube music, spotify, and apple music. it seeks
to make a device-native music experience regardless of what device or service
they use, and offer tools to manage and create local libraries for those who
prefer to own their music.

[[ pawprint ]]

pawprint is a loose idea i have for a "smartwatch" type device with no screen,
it is worn like a wristband and has as many health-related sensors as we can
cram into it. its primary use is as a pair to harm-reduction mobile apps which
intake various biological data, drug intake data, and physical activity data
and train a neural network on-device to help assess danger levels, detect
specific side effects of drug use, and advise on when danger levels are too
high and the user should attempt to get to a hospital or someone nearby should
administer something such as narcan. it also hopes to (consensually) build a
community database of anonymized health data that can be used to improve 
preexising harm reduction resources.
